Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with your order. This policy explains when you are entitled to a refund, how to ask for one and how long it takes. It works together with our Delivery & Returns page and our Terms & Conditions.

When you get a full refund

  • Faulty, damaged or wrong item: tell us within 7 days of delivery. You get a full refund (or a replacement if you prefer), including delivery costs.
  • Item out of stock after you paid: full refund, or an alternative if you prefer.
  • Order not delivered: if we cannot deliver your order, you get a full refund.
  • Defective goods: under the Consumer Protection Act you may return defective goods within six months for a repair, replacement or refund.

Change of mind

  • Unopened items in their original sealed packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • You pay the cost of sending the item back. We refund the price of the item once we receive it in its original condition.
  • For hygiene reasons we cannot refund fragrances that have been opened or used, unless they are faulty.

Payments that did not go through

If your card or Instant EFT payment failed or you cancelled it on PayFast, no money was taken and there is nothing to refund. If money left your account but your order shows as unpaid, contact us with your order reference and we will sort it out.

How to ask for a refund

  1. WhatsApp [WhatsApp number] or email [email address] with your order reference (it starts with ORD-) and a photo if the item is faulty or damaged.
  2. We reply within 2 working days and tell you where to send the item, if it needs to come back.
  3. Once we receive and check the item, we confirm your refund.

How and when you are paid

  • Paid by card or Instant EFT (PayFast): refunded through PayFast to the same card or account.
  • Paid by bank transfer: refunded by bank transfer to the account you give us.
  • Refunds are paid within 10 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.
